## REG-1 Purpose

Summarises the EU-level legal baseline that drives customer onboarding. National
transposition and supervisory rules add to or tighten this baseline; record them
in `10-country-overlays.md`.

## REG-3 Key AMLD obligations used by the agents

| ID | Obligation (AMLD 2015/849 reference) | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| REG-3.1 | CDD measures (Art. 13) | Identify and verify the customer; identify the beneficial owner and take reasonable measures to verify; understand ownership and control of legal entities; assess purpose and intended nature of the relationship; ongoing monitoring. Verify that a person acting on behalf of the customer is authorised and identify and verify that person. |
| REG-3.2 | Timing (Art. 14) | Verification before establishing the relationship. Member States may allow an account to be opened before verification is complete, provided no transactions are carried out until CDD is complete `[COUNTRY OVERLAY]`. If CDD cannot be completed, do not establish the relationship and consider a suspicious transaction report. |
| REG-3.3 | Simplified due diligence (Arts. 15–17) | Only where the bank's risk assessment identifies lower risk; never where ML/TF is suspected. |
| REG-3.4 | Enhanced due diligence (Arts. 18–24) | Higher-risk situations, high-risk third countries (Art. 18a), PEPs (Arts. 20–23), complex or unusually large transactions. |
| REG-3.5 | PEPs (Arts. 3(9)–(11), 20–23) | Relevant senior-management, source-of-funds/wealth and monitoring measures; family/associate/former-PEP handling needs the applicable legal and country policy. No period is configured here. |
| REG-3.6 | Beneficial ownership (Art. 3(6), Art. 30) | Apply the selected legal ownership/control tests, not a generic numerical default. Fallback conditions, recorded steps, register access and discrepancy duties need authorized national review. |
| REG-3.7 | Reporting and tipping off (Arts. 33, 39) | Suspicious transaction reports to the national FIU; prohibition on disclosing to the customer or third parties that a report or analysis is under way. |
| REG-3.8 | Record keeping (Art. 40) | Set the actual schedule from applicable national AML law, GDPR, legal holds and record category; this pack configures no retention duration. |
| REG-3.9 | Reliance on third parties (Arts. 25–29) | The bank remains ultimately responsible; reliance must be approved by policy `[BANK POLICY]`. |

## REG-4 Coming changes — EU AML package (adopted 2024)

| ID | Instrument | Status |
|---|---|---|
| REG-4.1 | Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 (AMLR) | General application **10 July 2027**, Art 90; not today's configured bank CDD rules. |
| REG-4.2 | Directive (EU) 2024/1640 | General transposition **10 July 2027**, with earlier 2025/2026 and later 2029 provisions; see source register for exact Art 78 phases. Not wholly future-only. |
| REG-4.3 | AMLA institutional transition | AMLA confirms mandate handover from EBA on **1 January 2026**; existing EBA standards/guidelines continue until replaced. Verify replacements before release. |

Agents must use the approved policy version only within its actual legal scope
and application dates. A bank cannot opt out of applicable law by keeping an
old policy version. Conflicts or missing overlays block conclusions and require
Compliance/Legal review. No ownership threshold is supplied by this pack.
