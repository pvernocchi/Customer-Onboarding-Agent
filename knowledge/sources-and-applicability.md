## What applies when

applicable customer-onboarding rulebook. Entry into force, application,
transposition and national implementation are different concepts.

## Release controls

Compliance must assign an accountable reviewer, assess subsequent amendments,
delegated/implementing acts, AMLA guidelines, national supervisory directions
and EEA adaptations, and record a signed-off applicability matrix. Use actual
legal-entity and jurisdiction coverage, not an unsupported "EU-wide" flag.
The pack sets **no mandatory ownership percentage, retention duration, refresh
interval, risk score, screening match threshold or universal evidence age**.
Those are unresolved policy/legal decisions, not missing values a model may fill.

Recommended workflow controls (case locking, reference-only results, manual
resume, model draft-only outputs) are explicitly engineering/control choices;
they are not represented as legal prescriptions in S1-S8. Conflicts or unclear
applicability block automated conclusions and go to authorized Compliance/Legal.
